Privacy Policy

Pulse monitors the websites you choose and notifies you when they go down. This page explains exactly what data we collect, why, and how it’s stored.

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Applies to the Pulse mobile app

The short version. Pulse does not require an account. We identify your install with a random, device-generated ID. We store the websites you add and the notification token needed to alert you. We don’t sell data, don’t track you across apps, and don’t collect anything beyond what’s needed to make the app work.

1. Who we are

Pulse is developed and operated by Elexoft (“we”, “us”, “our”). If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of your rights, contact us at privacy@elexoft.com.

2. What we collect

We collect only what’s necessary to run the monitoring service:

Data
What it is
Why we need it
Device identifier
A random ID generated by your device on first launch. It’s the same ID after a reinstall, so your data comes back.
To associate your sites with your install — no login required.
Website list
The name and URL of each site you add to monitor.
To actually check your sites every hour.
Notification token
A push token from Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
To deliver the “site is down” alert to your device.
Site status history
Whether each of your sites was up or down, and when it last changed.
To trigger alerts only when the status actually flips.

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or microphone
  • Your device’s IMEI, MAC address, or advertising identifier
  • Browsing history or activity outside the app

3. How it’s stored

All app data is stored on Supabase, a managed database platform. Supabase hosts data in secure data centers with encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Your device identifier is stored alongside your website list so we can return it to you after a reinstall.

The device identifier is generated locally on your device and does not leave your device except as part of the API calls needed to save and retrieve your own data.

4. How we use it

We use the data we collect only for:

  • Checking each site you add to the app
  • Sending you a push notification when a site goes down or recovers
  • Loading your saved sites back when you reinstall or open the app
  • Debugging failures — for example, seeing which checks are erroring out

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the operation of the app.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We share it only with the infrastructure providers required to run the service:

Supabase — database & API hosting Firebase Cloud Messaging — push delivery Google Play / Apple App Store — app distribution

Each provider is bound by their own privacy commitments and receives only the minimum data needed. We do not share your identifier with any third party for marketing or analytics purposes.

6. Retention & deletion

Your data lives on Supabase for as long as your device keeps using the app. If you uninstall Pulse and never return, we may delete your data after 12 months of inactivity. If you reinstall within that window, your list comes back automatically.

If you’d like your data deleted now, email privacy@elexoft.com with the approximate dates you used the app — we’ll delete everything within 7 days. Because we don’t have your email or name, deletion is done by matching your device identifier; contact us and we’ll guide you through it.

7. Security

Your device identifier is the only credential the app uses. It’s stored on your device in the system keychain (iOS) or encrypted shared preferences (Android). All network traffic is encrypted in transit. Because no login is required, the device identifier is what protects your data — don’t share it with anyone.

8. Children

Pulse isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we’ll delete the associated data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we’ll update the date at the top of this page and, where possible, notify you in-app. Continued use of Pulse after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns: privacy@elexoft.com
